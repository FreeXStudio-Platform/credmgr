# Support

CredMgr is built and maintained by the FreexStudio team. This page says where to ask for help and
what to expect.

## Where to ask

| You have | Go to |
|---|---|
| A bug, or something that does not work | A [GitHub issue](https://github.com/FreeXStudio-Platform/credmgr/issues/new/choose). Use the template, and never paste a real credential. |
| A security problem | Not a public issue. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). |
| A licensing question, a request for permission the [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) does not give, or a commercial arrangement | Admin@freexstudio.com |
| A donation proof (your transaction hash, never a key or seed phrase) | Admin@freexstudio.com. The addresses and the checks to make first are in [`v2/DONATE.md`](v2/DONATE.md). |
| A question about contributing | [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) |

## What is and is not supported

- Pre-release versions, including the current `2.0.0-beta.1`, are best-effort. There is no service
  level, no response-time promise and no phone or chat support.
- Only the current stable version, as published unchanged by FreexStudio, is approved for use under
  the [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). Modified, forked or unofficial builds are not supported.
- Your vault lives on your device and FreexStudio cannot open it or recover a lost password or recovery
  code. Keep your own backups.

## Keeping the project going

CredMgr is free to use today. Future versions may not be free, and no date or price is promised. What
helps it continue: running the current release, reporting problems with a clear description, and, if
you wish, a voluntary donation ([`v2/DONATE.md`](v2/DONATE.md)). A donation is a gift; it buys no
rights, support or features.
