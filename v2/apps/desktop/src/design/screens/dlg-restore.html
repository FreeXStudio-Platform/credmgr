rebuilds a vault from an archive made by Back up

The archive is checked whole before anything is written, and it is written into the folder all at once or not at all. A restore never merges into, or overwrites, a vault, and it never runs beside a running broker. The passphrase is the backup's, not the vault's; it is not kept.
This window is connected to a running broker, so a restore from here is refused. Quit CredMgr first: the screen it shows when no broker answers offers this restore, or use credmgr restore in a terminal.
